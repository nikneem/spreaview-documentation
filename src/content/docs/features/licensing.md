SpreaView offers three types of licenses to cater to various conference needs. Each license comes with specific capabilities and limitations, allowing organizers to choose the most suitable option for their events.

## Community License

The Community License is designed for free events and provides an easy way for organizers to gather speaker feedback without additional costs.

**Key Features:**

- Ideal for events that are completely free for all attendees.
- Free to use with no activation costs.
- Instant activation available directly through the SpreaView platform.

**Limitations:**

- The event must be entirely free for all participants.
- Limited to basic features, such as generating QR codes and accessing fundamental review dashboards.

This license is perfect for community-driven events, small meetups, or non-profit gatherings.

## Pro License

The Pro License is tailored for professional events that may charge attendance fees or require more advanced features.

**Key Features:**

- Suitable for paid or free events.
- Includes advanced analytics for detailed insights into speaker and session performance.
- Allows customization of feedback forms, including enabling/disabling the optional free comment field.
- Priority email support for event organizers.

**Limitations:**

- Requires a subscription fee based on the number of events or attendees.

This license is ideal for mid-sized conferences, workshops, or any event requiring professional-grade feedback tools.

## Premium License

The Premium License is designed for large-scale conferences or organizations that require full customization and integration capabilities.

**Key Features:**

- All features of the Pro License, plus:
  - Custom branding for feedback interfaces and dashboards.
  - Dedicated account manager and 24/7 support.
  - Bulk licensing options for multiple events.

This license is best suited for organizations running large conferences.

## Custom License

The Custom License is tailored for organizers who run multiple conferences annually or require unique capabilities beyond the standard license offerings.

**Key Features:**

- Fully customizable options to meet specific event needs.
- Flexible pricing based on usage and requirements.
- Personalized support to design and implement custom features.

Organizers interested in a Custom License should contact our support team at [support@spreaview.com](mailto:support@spreaview.com) for more information.

## How to Acquire a License

To acquire a license, organizers should create a new conference in SpreaView. On the conference details page, there is a button that allows for acquiring a license. Once a license is acquired, you can review the license on the Licenses page of SpreaView. This is also where you can pay for your license and/or activate the license. You can change the license for your conference up until the license is paid and activated.

## Choosing the Right License

To determine which license is right for your event, consider:

- **Event Type:** Is your event free or paid?
- **Event Size:** How many attendees and speakers are expected?
- **Feature Needs:** Do you need basic feedback tools or advanced analytics and customization?
