SpreaView offers three types of licenses to suit the needs of different conferences: **Free**, **Pro**, and **Premium**. Each license comes with its own features and limitations, designed to accommodate a wide range of events.

## License Types

### Free License

- **Type**: Community License
- **Ideal For**: Free events open to the public.
- **Limitations**:
  - Maximum of **2 tracks**.
  - Limited to **1,000 review entries**.

### Pro License

- **Type**: Paid License
- **Ideal For**: Small to medium-sized conferences with moderate review volume.
- **Limitations**:
  - Maximum of **5 simultaneous tracks**.
  - Limited to **1,500 review results**.

### Premium License

- **Type**: Paid License
- **Ideal For**: Large-scale conferences with extensive review needs.
- **Limitations**:
  - Supports up to **10 simultaneous tracks**.
  - Unlimited review results.

## Custom Plans

If none of these licenses fully meet the needs of your conference, or if you organize multiple conferences annually, please contact us at **support@spreaview.com**. We are happy to work with you to create a tailored plan that fits your requirements.

## Acquiring a License

![Aquire a license](/images/spreaview-licensing-aquire.png)

To acquire a license for a conference, follow these steps:

1. **Open Conference Details**

   - Navigate to the details page of the conference that requires a license.

2. **Click the "Acquire License" Button**

   - This button is only visible for conferences without an active license.

3. **Select a License**
   - In the popup, choose the appropriate license for your event.
   - This action will create a **trial license**, which needs to be activated later.

## Managing Licenses

![Manage licenses](/images/spreaview-licensing-list.png)

To manage your licenses:

1. **Navigate to the Licenses Section**

   - Click the **Licenses** menu item in the purple section.

2. **View Licenses**

   - See an overview of licenses for all conferences under the selected organization.

3. **Activate or Pay for Licenses**
   - Activate trial licenses or complete payment for paid licenses.

![Manage licenses](/images/spreaview-licensing-details.png)

> Note that at this time, paying for a license is not available from the SpreaView website. Contact support@spreaview.com in order to pay for your license so that you can activate it.

## License Activation and Review Dashboard Access

The **Review Dashboard** for a conference is only accessible when the conference has an activated license. Even free community licenses must be activated to access and manage review data.

## Support

For any questions about licenses, custom plans, or other licensing-related matters, contact us at **support@spreaview.com**. We are here to help ensure that SpreaView meets the demands of your events.
