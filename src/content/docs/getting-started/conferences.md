In SpreaView, a **conference** serves as the central structure that combines and organizes speakers, presentations, and tracks. Conferences provide a comprehensive framework for setting up and managing events, including customization, integration, and advanced topic analysis.

## What Is a Conference?

A conference in SpreaView groups together key elements like speakers, presentations, and tracks to create a cohesive event. It includes several customizable features to tailor the attendee and organizer experience:

- **Event Scheduling**: Define the dates and times during which the conference takes place.
- **Theming Options**: Adjust the appearance of presentation review pages to align with the conference’s branding or theme.
- **Review Configuration**: Enable or disable the optional free comment field on review pages.

## Sessionize Integration

SpreaView offers seamless integration with Sessionize, a platform commonly used to manage conference schedules.

**Key Features:**

- **Sessionize API Key**: Assign a Sessionize API Key to your conference to enable integration.
- **Data Import and Synchronization**: Import and sync speakers, presentations, and tracks directly from Sessionize. This dramatically reduces setup time for organizers who use Sessionize to manage their conference schedules.
- **Efficiency**: Avoid manual data entry and ensure your SpreaView setup mirrors your Sessionize configuration.

## Topic Configuration

To enhance organization and analysis, organizers can configure a list of topics their conference covers.

**Key Details:**

- **Custom Topic List**: For optimal results, define a list of approximately 30 topics for larger conferences. Smaller conferences may require fewer topics.
- **AI-Assisted Topic Matching**: When creating or adjusting presentations, SpreaView’s AI interprets the presentation abstract and matches it to the predefined topics.
- **Fallback Behavior**: If no topics are provided, the AI generates a broader and less accurate variety of topics, potentially leading to inconsistencies in data.

## Managing Your Conference

Organizers can efficiently manage their conferences in SpreaView by:

1. **Setting Up the Basics**: Define the conference’s schedule, theme, and review configurations.
2. **Integrating with Sessionize**: Use the API Key to import and synchronize data quickly.
3. **Defining Topics**: Configure a topic list for more accurate AI-driven categorization.
4. **Assigning Elements**: Link speakers, presentations, and tracks to create a well-structured schedule.

By leveraging these features, SpreaView enables a streamlined and powerful approach to conference management.
