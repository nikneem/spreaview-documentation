# Understanding Tracks in SpreaView

In SpreaView, a **track** is a structural element of a conference that helps in organizing and categorizing sessions or presentations. Tracks play a crucial role in defining how a conference's schedule is divided and how attendees can navigate the event.

## What Is a Track?

A track represents a specific division within a conference. Depending on the conference type and size, a track can be:

- **A Room**: In smaller conferences, a track might correspond to a single room where all sessions or presentations are hosted sequentially.
- **An Area**: For larger conferences, a track might be a physical or thematic area hosting simultaneous sessions.
- **A Theme**: Tracks can also represent thematic categories, such as "Technology", "Leadership", or "Innovation", to group sessions with related content.

## Why Use Tracks?

Tracks enhance the organization of a conference by:

- **Streamlining Scheduling**: Tracks allow sessions to run concurrently in separate locations or areas without overlap or confusion.
- **Improving Navigation**: Attendees can easily find sessions they are interested in by filtering the schedule by track.
- **Enabling Flexibility**: Organizers can tailor tracks to match the scale and goals of the event, whether it’s a single-track meetup or a multi-track conference.

## Examples of Tracks in Action

1. **Single-Track Event**:

   - A small conference or workshop might consist of one track where all attendees follow the same schedule in one room.

2. **Multi-Track Event**:

   - A large tech conference may have multiple tracks, such as "Frontend Development", "Backend Development", and "DevOps", with each track hosting sessions in separate rooms.

3. **Hybrid Tracks**:
   - An event might organize tracks based on a combination of rooms and themes, such as "Main Hall" and "Breakout Sessions" for physical locations, or "Innovation" and "Networking" for thematic groupings.

## Track-Specific URLs for Reviews

Each track in SpreaView is assigned a unique, track-specific URL for reviewing the session that is currently active on that track. Over the course of the day, the same URL will dynamically update to display the appropriate review page for the active session.

**Key Benefits:**

- **Convenience for Organizers**: Organizers can print review QR codes linked to these URLs and place them in the vicinity of the track, such as near entrances or on walls.
- **Simplified Feedback Collection**: Attendees don’t need to search for session-specific links; scanning the QR code always leads to the correct active session.

This feature makes it easy to gather session-specific feedback while maintaining a clean and efficient workflow for both organizers and attendees.

## Managing Tracks in SpreaView

Tracks are integral to the conference setup process in SpreaView. Organizers can:

1. **Define Tracks**: Specify the number and type of tracks when creating the conference.
2. **Assign Sessions**: Link each session or presentation to a specific track to create a clear and navigable schedule.
3. **Customize Tracks**: Adjust track names and details to match the conference’s structure and goals.

By leveraging tracks effectively, organizers can ensure a seamless and engaging experience for both speakers and attendees.
