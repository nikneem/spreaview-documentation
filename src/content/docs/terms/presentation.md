In SpreaView, a **presentation** is a core element of a conference, serving as the primary subject for attendee reviews. Presentations provide structured information delivered by speakers and are key to the success of any conference.

## What Is a Presentation?

A presentation is an organized session where a speaker (or speakers) conveys information to an audience. It is defined by several attributes:

- **Association with a Conference**: Every presentation is part of a specific conference and contributes to its overall agenda.
- **Speakers**: Each presentation is assigned at least one speaker but may have multiple speakers.
- **Scheduled Time**: Presentations are always planned for a specific date and time, ensuring clarity in the conference schedule.
- **Assigned Track**: Every presentation is assigned to a track, which determines where and under which category the session takes place.

## Review Time Period for Presentations

To maintain accurate and timely feedback, each presentation in SpreaView has a designated review time period. This feature ensures that only relevant attendee reviews are processed.

**Key Details:**

- **Default Time-Frame**: By default, the review period lasts 24 hours, starting exactly at the same time as the presentation begins.
- **Customizable Settings**: Depending on the conference settings, the review time period may be adjusted to suit the needs of the event.
- **Review Processing**: Reviews submitted within the allowed time-frame are processed and included in the feedback reports. Any reviews submitted outside this period are ignored.

## Importance of Presentations

Presentations are central to the attendee experience and provide:

- **Knowledge Sharing**: A platform for speakers to share insights, ideas, and expertise.
- **Engagement**: An opportunity for attendees to learn, ask questions, and interact with speakers.
- **Feedback Opportunities**: A structured way for attendees to share their opinions, helping organizers and speakers improve future events.

## Managing Presentations in SpreaView

Organizers can easily manage presentations in SpreaView by:

1. **Creating Presentations**: Define presentations under the appropriate conference, including title, speakers, and schedule.
2. **Assigning Tracks**: Link presentations to tracks for clear organization within the conference.
3. **Adjusting Review Settings**: Customize the review time period as needed to align with conference goals.
